    Grid-study identifier
    & \texttt{prti-grid-k5-30-c2-20-p100-i100-v1}. \\

    MO-OBAM reference
    & arXiv:2501.01002v2, revision dated 15 May 2025. \\

    Grid-study output policy
    & Aggregate checkpoints and summary evidence only; patient-level outputs
    were disabled during parameter comparison. \\

    Final-output policy
    & Patient-level anonymized output was generated only for the selected
    configuration in the separate held-out run. \\

    \midrule
    \multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Parameter-grid settings}}\\*
    \midrule

    Minimum cluster size, $k$
    & Every integer from 5 to 30 inclusive (26 values). \\

    Number of clusters, $n_C$
    & Every integer from 2 to 20 inclusive (19 values). \\

    Diversity weight, $\lambda$
    & $0,\;10^{-7},\;2\times10^{-7},\;10^{-6},$
    \newline
    $10^{-5},\;2\times10^{-5},\;10^{-4},\;2\times10^{-4},$
    \newline
    $5\times10^{-4},\;10^{-3},\;0.0025,\;0.005,\;0.01,$
    \newline
    $0.025,\;0.05,\;0.1,\;0.25,\;0.5,\;0.75,\;1$
    (20 values). \\

    Comparison seeds
    & 0, 1, and 2. \\

    Held-out seed
    & 12; excluded from parameter selection and reserved for one final
    evaluation of the selected configuration. \\

    Planned configurations
    & $26\times19\times20=9{,}880$. \\

    Planned comparison runs
    & $9{,}880\times3=29{,}640$. \\

    Fixed $\ell$-diversity setting
    & No fixed $\ell$-diversity constraint was imposed. Distinct and
    entropy-effective diversity were designated as post-anonymization
    evaluation measures. \\

    \midrule
    \multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{PSO and execution settings}}\\*
    \midrule

    PSO population size
    & 100 particles per run. \\

    PSO iterations
    & 100 iterations per run. \\

    Cognitive/social random-coefficient maximum
    & 0.01. \\

    Population initialization policy
    & One randomly initialized particle population per configuration--seed
    run; no automatic reinitialization was applied when that population
    contained no particle satisfying hard $k$-anonymity. \\

    Configured process-worker pool
    & Maximum of 32 worker processes; this is the configured pool size, not a
    claim that 32 CPU cores were simultaneously utilized. \\

    Worker backend
    & Independent process workers. \\

    Numerical-library thread limits
    & \texttt{OMP\_NUM\_THREADS=1},
    \texttt{OPENBLAS\_NUM\_THREADS=1},
    \texttt{MKL\_NUM\_THREADS=1}, and
    \texttt{NUMEXPR\_NUM\_THREADS=1}. \\

    Checkpoint reuse for the declared grid
    & Disabled at study creation (\texttt{--no-reuse}); matching checkpoints
    created by the study were subsequently used to resume unfinished work. \\

    \midrule
    \multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Screening settings}}\\*
    \midrule

    Required comparison runs
    & All three comparison seeds must complete for a configuration to be
    eligible. \\

    Cluster-size requirement
    & Every configured cluster must contain at least $k$ records in every
    comparison run. \\

    Maximum information loss
    & $IL\leq0.60$ in every comparison run. \\

    Maximum homogeneity fraction
    & At most 0.20 (20\%) of records in every comparison run. \\

    Maximum zero-entropy sensitive attributes
    & At most one sensitive attribute in every comparison run. \\

    Linkage thresholds
    & $\tau\in\{0.05,\,0.075,\,0.10\}$. \\

    Maximum linkage flags
    & Zero records at every configured linkage threshold in every comparison
    run. \\

    \midrule
    \multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textbf{Software and provenance settings}}\\*
    \midrule

    Python

    & Two Intel Xeon Gold 6230R processors at 2.10\,GHz, with 26 physical

    Host logical CPUs

    System memory
    & 93\,GiB RAM and 8\,GiB swap. \\

    Grid-selection evidence date

    & 8 November 2026 (completed at 01:50:04 UTC). \\

    Git base commit for the held-out run

    Code-snapshot qualification
    & The held-out run began from a working tree containing 11 changed paths.
    Exact SHA-256 hashes of generation and validation files were captured in
    the final artifact manifest. \\
